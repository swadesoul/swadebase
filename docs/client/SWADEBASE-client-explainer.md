# SWADEBASE: a business system I build for you (read-aloud version)

**The short answer**

SWADEBASE is the operating system I built to run my own businesses, and now I set it up for other companies under their name. It's one private headquarters for a company's websites, apps, logins, data and AI helpers. You don't sign up for my service. You get your own version of the system, branded as yours, with the pieces you need turned on and the ones you don't turned off.

**Why it exists**

Most companies are spread thin. The website is in one place, the customer list is in another, the passwords are in someone's head, and every new tool is another monthly bill and another thing that can break. When something goes wrong, nobody knows where to look, and when someone leaves, the knowledge walks out with them. I built SWADEBASE to fix that for myself, and it works, so now I build it for others.

**What the whole system does**

- **One locker for everything.** All the keys, logins and settings live in one secure place, and every site and app pulls from it. Nothing gets set up twice and nothing gets lost.
- **It watches itself.** It checks every site and service around the clock, tries to fix problems on its own first, and tells you exactly what broke if it can't.
- **Pieces with an on/off switch.** Every tool plugs in the same way, and each one has its own switch. If something misbehaves, you turn off that one piece without taking the business down.
- **A memory that doesn't walk out the door.** Every day it writes down what was done and why, so the next person or the next AI picks up right where the last one left off.
- **An AI team that works inside it.** The AI helpers know the business, follow its rules, and ask for approval before anything important.
- **A research lab.** It can study how a feature works on another website or app, show the evidence, and turn that into a plan to build something similar.
- **A business advisor.** It tracks the company's entities, deadlines and paperwork, and points out what you might be missing.

**Same core, shaped to your industry**

The foundation is the same for everyone. What changes is which pieces are switched on and how they're set up for your kind of business.

- **A general business:** websites, customer records, the AI team, paperwork tracking and the daily memory, all under your brand.
- **A record company:** artist and catalog management, music uploads, release planning, and the tools to promote and sell music.
- **A video company:** video storage and publishing, transcripts and captions, content planning, and distribution to your channels.
- **A company focused on making money:** stores and checkout, subscriptions, sales tracking, and an AI team pointed at finding and growing revenue.
- **A real estate company:** property listings, lead and client tracking, documents and deadlines, and follow-up handled by the AI team.

If you don't need something, it stays off. You don't pay for it, it doesn't clutter your screen, and it can't break.

**How I set it up for you**

Everything I've built and run on my own servers is being turned into a repeatable playbook. That means I can copy the whole system for a new client, put your name on it, and set the gates so you only get the programs you actually need. A new client isn't a from-scratch build. It's the proven system, duplicated and fitted to you.

**What you get out of it**

- One home for your whole operation, owned under your name instead of rented from a dozen companies.
- Fewer monthly software bills.
- Sites and apps that stay up and fix themselves.
- Passwords and private information kept out of emails and sticky notes.
- Faster launches, because the foundation is already built.
- AI doing real work for you, with a human approving the big decisions.

**The one-liner**

"SWADEBASE is the business headquarters I built for myself, and I set up your own version of it, under your name, with exactly the pieces your business needs turned on."
