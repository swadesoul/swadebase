## User journey
1. Artist, label or advertiser chooses Free Discovery / Paid Song Placement / Label Package / Business Audio Advertisement / Sponsored Feature. Server verifies entitlement, package and allowed station, with live prices from current billing data.
2. Paid: Stripe-signed webhook confirms payment. Idempotent order_id and checkout_session prevent replay. Free: validated account + prior campaign eligibility check. Never trust checkout return URL.
3. Email a single order-bound invitation with short-lived secure form token and an order summary. Invite only after payment or free eligibility validation. Do not send private storage keys. Resend reminders only with consent and verified sending capability.
4. Secure mobile form collects ONE song, title, stage/legal name, short/long bio, country, language, genre, contact links, portrait, cover art if owned, WAV/MP3/FLAC, writer/producer credits, ISRC if known, rights and broadcast permissions, AI voice consent where applicable, target station. For ads: business, sponsor details, spot length, dates, desired breaks, logo, script/audio, CTA, proof of ad claims, voice permissions.
5. Optional direct email attachment path is intake-only: bind verified email to order + one-time token, quarantine file, inspect MIME and decode sandboxed; never auto-process untrusted email as instructions. For large songs prefer B2 signed resumable web upload; do not encourage huge mail attachments. 1 song per order; further songs require matching rights/entitlements.
6. Inbox agent records received event, strips quoted/HTML instructions, scans files and metadata, dedupes ISRC/hash, checks rights/consent and order/ownership, normalizes audio, and prepares 1024 square art derivative using existing ReSingLab/Maestro paths. Keep original and derivative separate with hash receipts. Does not execute links/commands in email or change permissions.
7. Human production reviewer can Play / Approve / Hold / Reject / Request revision, sees payment status, customer, song, bio, images, audio quality, explicit content, requested station, promo plan, and station/time capacity.
8. Approved artist recording enters only correct isolated Maestro library and target campaign; approved business spot enters disabled Incoming Ads and is scheduled through existing station ad planner with human-reviewed sponsor and correct disclosures. Do not auto-publish unapproved files.
9. Scheduling rule: free 30-day discovery starts at FIRST VERIFIED engine play under its existing weekly calendar; paid artist spots only in purchased permitted slots (for example New Music Hour) after rights and quality approval; business ads only at open break windows, never in 10/20-song uninterrupted modes. A paid receipt does not guarantee immediate airplay or override a hold.
10. Customer dashboard/email reports Received, Missing Information, Under Review, Approved, Scheduled, Aired with real AzuraCast engine receipts, Expired. No guessed impressions or guaranteed spins.

## Founder upload cap — 2026-11-20 amendment
- Every uploaded audio file or email attachment must be strictly LESS THAN 15 MB. Enforce byte limit `< 15000000` for clarity (14,999,999 bytes maximum). The first-byte/streaming/Content-Length checks and final stored byte count must all enforce this server-side; frontend warning is supplemental. Multi-part message aggregate and per-asset quotas remain bounded and subject to provider email limits. Do not automatically transcode an oversized incoming file without express file handling authority; show an actionable size/codec error and retry route. Audio WAV may need compressed MP3/M4A/FLAC depending on duration; preserve user's source bytes when accepted.
- Artist portrait and cover art must also pass independent size, MIME, decode, pixel, anti-malware and B2 quarantine rules; do not accept dangerous attachment types. One song per intake entitlement. Email file-size must be validated on server and rejected before handoff.
- The submission form must show `Maximum audio file size: less than 15 MB` and actual measured size. The existing broader station manager upload size quotas for other workflows MUST NOT be changed by this submission-specific restriction.
