Choose the category that best describes the item or right. If you are unsure, preserve its exact wording and explain the uncertainty.

## Understanding a bond

A bond describes a debt obligation under stated terms. Its printed face amount is not the same as current sale value or a confirmed payable balance.

Identify the issuer, exact issue, holder, maturity and current status. Obtain the appropriate issuer, registrar or custody evidence before relying on the claim.

An old or convincing certificate may require corporate-action, estate or redemption checks.

Related public articles: AM-BANK-INSTRUMENTS-002, AM-INHERITED-AND-PHYSICAL-ASSETS-004.

## What an SKR establishes

An SKR is a safekeeping receipt whose exact custody terms need checking. A receipt alone does not establish the contents, ownership, value or financing availability.

Ask what was received, whether it was inspected, what is held now, who may obtain delivery and which charges or claims apply.

Connect the receipt, custody agreement and identified property through direct confirmation from the appropriate custodian.

Related public articles: AM-BANK-INSTRUMENTS-003, AM-BANK-INSTRUMENTS-007.

## Understanding an SBLC

An SBLC is a bank undertaking with specific terms and drawing conditions. Its stated amount is not unrestricted cash available to the beneficiary.

Identify the applicant, issuing bank, beneficiary, expiry, applicable terms and any advising or confirming bank. Verification should reach the bank through an independently established channel.

A message label or supplied SWIFT copy does not settle the undertaking, current availability or a lender’s decision.

Related public articles: AM-BANK-INSTRUMENTS-004, AM-BANK-INSTRUMENTS-005.

## Inherited documents and authority

Inherited papers can be useful evidence, but the right to act for an estate or transfer an asset must also be established.

Preserve originals and identify the asset’s registration, the relevant estate or trust, and the person currently authorized to act.

A will, beneficiary arrangement, trust and court appointment can have different roles. The applicable process depends on the asset and jurisdiction.

Related public articles: AM-INHERITED-AND-PHYSICAL-ASSETS-001, AM-INHERITED-AND-PHYSICAL-ASSETS-002, AM-INHERITED-AND-PHYSICAL-ASSETS-007.

## Gold: identity, custody and value

Identify whether the claim concerns physical metal, an account entitlement or another right. Testing, ownership, custody and valuation answer different questions.

Match bar or item identifiers, weight, stated fineness, inspection records and custody evidence. Use a test method suitable for the item and the claim.

A photograph, hallmark or price calculation alone does not establish the metal’s composition or availability for delivery.

Related public articles: AM-INHERITED-AND-PHYSICAL-ASSETS-008, AM-INHERITED-AND-PHYSICAL-ASSETS-009, AM-INHERITED-AND-PHYSICAL-ASSETS-010.

## Gems: identify the actual stone

A gem report must be checked with its issuer and matched to the actual stone. Identification or grading is separate from ownership and achievable sale value.

Record the material, natural or laboratory-created origin where applicable, treatments, dimensions and identifying features.

A matching report number supports the report record; it does not alone establish that the seller holds the described stone.

Related public articles: AM-INHERITED-AND-PHYSICAL-ASSETS-014, AM-INHERITED-AND-PHYSICAL-ASSETS-015, AM-INHERITED-AND-PHYSICAL-ASSETS-016.

## Minerals and underground resources

A mineral deposit or mining interest is a different claim from metal already available for delivery. Review the rights and technical report that support the claim.

Record the report’s author, qualifications, effective date, reporting standard and assumptions, along with the relevant property or mineral rights.

Do not describe an underground resource estimate as a deliverable vault inventory.

Related public articles: AM-INHERITED-AND-PHYSICAL-ASSETS-005, AM-INHERITED-AND-PHYSICAL-ASSETS-013.

## When you do not know the name

Use the exact wording, markings and description you can observe. An unfamiliar label should remain a question until the item is properly classified.

Photograph both sides and preserve related records. Keep what someone told you separate from what the document or object actually shows.

Choose the appropriate specialist after clarifying the claim; do not infer a financial product from a nickname.

## Choose the appropriate professional

A securities desk, trade-finance team and bullion specialist perform different functions. Interest from a contact is not a financing commitment.

## What a wallet can show

A public address can reveal ledger information on a specific network. Seeing a balance does not establish who controls it, owns it or may transfer it.

Start with the exact network and public address. Separate observed balance, control evidence, legal title and economic value.

Never provide private keys or recovery phrases to establish an asset claim.

## Review the exact smart contract

A token name or ticker is not enough to identify a contract. Record its network and exact address, then examine the relevant controls and restrictions.

The review may need to identify who can mint, pause, freeze, change fees or upgrade the contract. A proxy can point to separate implementation code.

A contract or ledger observation does not by itself establish off-chain asset backing or legal rights.

## What a token represents

Tokenization records or represents a defined right. The holder’s enforceable rights, responsible parties and redemption terms need to be clear.

Review the underlying asset, authority, custody, restrictions and proposed holder rights before considering issuance.

Creating a digital record does not establish the underlying asset’s authenticity, value or acceptance by a lender.

## Keep evidence current

## What an oracle can establish

An oracle relays information or an attestation from a source. Its output depends on that source’s scope and freshness.

Ask who made the assertion, what was checked, when it was checked and how it relates to the particular asset.

An oracle is not independent bond authentication, a valuation guarantee or a funding commitment. External attestation feeds are currently awaiting connection.
