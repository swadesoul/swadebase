# Asset Machine Master Site Bible
Version 0.1 — October 26, 2026. DESIGN CONCEPTS ONLY.
Founder named this separate bond/asset platform Asset Machine. It is not Cochrane. Existing Asset Review core at https://swadebase.cloud/adapters/asset-review/ remains a possible reusable foundation; it was not renamed or represented as the new Asset Machine release.
Three cinematic generated page concepts delivered: homepage; Verification Room; Digital Asset & Deal Room. Obsidian, titanium and copper, restrained teal evidence states, English-first EN/ES/FR control. Images are conceptual, records illustrative, not deployed screens or real counterparty/custody records.
Proposed workflow: issuer/evidence review -> ownership/restrictions -> custody -> legal-rights structure -> scoped oracle evidence -> approved issuance -> eligible counterparty -> funding/rights closing -> reconciliation/redemption/monitoring.
An oracle reports approved source data and freshness. It does not independently authenticate physical paper, establish title, value a bond, guarantee reserves or supply financing. Separate issuer confirmation, custodian control, valuation, legal review and technical audit statuses. ETH/USD reference-feed success is not a bond verification.
Specific claimed physical $100m Federal Reserve bonds overlap Treasury's published counterfeit warnings. Obtain issuer-level verification of exact instruments before treating face value as backing; collectible value and redeemable debt value are different.
For a real buyer/lender: signed terms, applicable eligibility checks, custody/control, legally effective ownership/security-entitlement or collateral records, funded escrow/settlement, matched rights transfer and payment, reconciliation afterward. Token transfer alone does not create legal rights or market liquidity. Sale, redemption and secured lending require actual counterparties/arrangements.
Minting must depend on approved backing and legal issuance limits, with stale/disputed evidence blocking new issuance and raising alerts. Redemption/default/repayment must follow documented rights; do not blindly freeze all redemptions because a feed is stale.
Proposed 2.1% success fee stays disabled pending a legally permitted arrangement. Danny's private legal notes are not investor-facing by default. Do not issue tokens, charge transaction fees, approach counterparties or send messages from concept approval alone.
Sources: Treasury OIG Examples of Known Phony Securities; SEC staff Statement on Tokenized Securities January 28, 2026 (staff view, not a binding rule); investor.gov Tokenized Securities; Chainlink Proof of Reserve and SecureMintPolicy documentation.
