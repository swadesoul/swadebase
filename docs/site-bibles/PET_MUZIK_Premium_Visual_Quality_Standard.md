# PET MUZIK premium visual quality standard

Founder directive: October 28, 2026, America/Chicago. PET MUZIK is premium merchandise for customers who expect exceptional quality. A convincing thumbnail is not acceptance. Automated completion times never override quality.

## Mandatory agent responsibilities

The Producer records the exact source revision and schedules a visual quality review before placement. The Character & Art Steward inspects the original at full resolution, close-up, and at the intended physical/display size. The Continuity / QA Guardian reviews the same immutable source and actual provider mockups; the generation model's self-description and a script reporting PASS are not visual evidence. The Merchandise Steward may upload or expose only assets whose checks and approvals permit that next step. All roles must retrieve this standard alongside the current approved source/brief. A saved document alone does not prove every deployed worker has loaded or enforced it.

## Source review — every image, every revision

1. Count limbs and verify attachment to the correct shoulder/hip; trace each visible limb through occlusions. Check species-appropriate joints, stance, weight balance, paw direction, toes, tails, ears, teeth and eyes. Hidden anatomy must be plausible, never explained away after seeing a defect. Stylization must be intentional and coherent.
2. Look for fused, duplicate, floating, warped, cut-off or extra anatomy and malformed props. Check grips, object intersections, perspective, accessory fasteners, shadows and material continuity.
3. Inspect eyes, expression, coat markings and likeness against the approved character reference. Preserve identity across every derivative. Inspect every character in multi-pet scenes separately.
4. Inspect cutout edges on white, black and mid-gray backgrounds at full size and enlarged close-up. Reject colored fringe, stray pixels, unintended holes, opaque rectangles, jagged fur and dirty alpha edges.
5. Check every printed word letter for letter: spelling, punctuation, hierarchy, kerning, contrast, intentional line breaks and legibility at actual product size. Inspect symbols and emblems as carefully as text.
6. Record original pixel dimensions and effective scale from verified provider geometry. Never call an enlarged low-detail source high resolution because its canvas or DPI metadata is large. Reject detail that will not reproduce well at the intended size.
7. Inspect composition, balanced negative space, palette and finish at both close-up and product scale. Premium approval is more than absence of obvious errors. Do not claim that everyone will like or buy a design.

## Placement and physical-product review

Use the actual provider, blueprint, method and each enabled variant's measured placeholder. Inspect real front/back/side provider mockups, seam and cut lines, bandana folds, bowl curvature, hat print boundaries, sleeve/cuff/waistband coverage, zipper interference and print contrast. Official safe/bleed geometry that has not been retrieved is unknown, not passed. AOP coverage must reach all actual fabric panels; never force placed-print accessories into AOP claims.

## Failure and immutable revision rules

Any visible defect => `BLOCKED_QUALITY` or a specific subtype such as `BLOCKED_ANATOMY`. Block every derivative of that source. Preserve the rejected master and reason, quarantine its placements, stop uploads/publication and repair as a new revision. Recheck the entire corrected asset because an edit can introduce new defects. Never overwrite the approved master or silently swap it. Record source SHA-256, corrected source SHA-256, change brief, reviewer, evidence, per-check outcomes and owner approval separately.

State transitions: generated → needs_visual_review → source_visually_reviewed → owner_approved → placement_review → mockup_reviewed → catalog_ready. A provider draft for review remains unpublished. Successful file dimensions, an image upload, a deadline, or a scheduled task is not approval.

## Required review receipt

Asset/revision/hash; reviewer and timestamp; original size; anatomy/identity/edges/text/resolution/composition outcomes; reviewed backgrounds and zoom levels; unresolved defects; exact provider/variant and physical mockups; owner approval reference; downstream products invalidated. Use pass/fail/pending/not-applicable, never a blanket fabricated pass.

## Regression example

October 28 First Press `good-intentions` v1 is REJECTED. It showed two forepaws holding a biscuit plus three apparent lower limbs, unclear hip attachment, and an incorrect lifted hind-paw angle. The owner identified the defect. All eight product adaptations are blocked. A corrected v2 must be visually inspected before reuse; it is not approved merely because generation completed. The other two First Press originals are also undergoing renewed full-size review and must not inherit approval from this correction.

## Daily automation

Produce three distinct designs on the calendar's one selected product type. Begin at 05:00 and target the Site by 08:00 America/Chicago, including daylight-saving transitions. A failed or pending review remains on hold beyond the deadline. Report actual completed/visible/held counts. No substituted artwork, invented QA, automatic purchasing, new external spending or lowered quality bar to meet the deadline.
